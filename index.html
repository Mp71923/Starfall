How to play

Move and fight

  • WASD or arrow keys to move, or drag on a touch screen
  • Your ship fires automatically
  • Space or B drops a bomb, V calls a summon, U opens run upgrades
  • P or Esc pauses, Z changes game speed (1x, 1.5x, 2x, 3x) in any mode except Campaign

Menus

  • Enter play, E game modes, C campaign
  • H hangar, T traits, Q quests, A achievements
  • G casino, X trade, I inventory, L log out
  • K save backup (export or import a save code), 5 replay the guide, N music on or off
  • 1 wingmen, 2 forge, 3 raids and trials, 4 profile, ranks and top scores
  • In those screens: arrows change tab and choice, Enter selects, Esc goes back

Display and sound

F toggles fullscreen from any menu, M mutes, ? opens this help.

The arrows button forces the game to stretch over the whole screen. Normally it only stretches when that looks natural, such as on phones.

The half-filled circle button turns off screen shake, white flashes and flickering. It starts on when your device asks for reduced motion.

Screen readers

Menus, waves, hearts left and results are announced. The playfield itself is visual.
